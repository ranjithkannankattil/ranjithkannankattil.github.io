Profile

Senior Project Engineer with 11+ years across the full project lifecycle — FEED, EPC tendering, constructability, execution planning and commissioning — on oil & gas and heavy-industrial infrastructure. Currently on ExxonMobil's Mozambique Rovuma LNG Phase 1 Integrated Project Team in Milan — widely regarded as the world's largest LNG project currently in development, built on a remote island site with no existing infrastructure. My work sits where engineering meets the contractor — constructability reviews, technical bid evaluation, weight management and strategic milestone coordination, and EPC tender packages large enough that major international contractors (Saipem, McDermott, Daewoo, CPECC, TEN, JGC) competed for them. Across my career I've supervised over 10 million manhours on site and led execution planning for more than 120 million manhours of project scope, across five major EPC projects. Before ExxonMobil: eight years across owner, EPC-contractor and consulting roles in India — a ~$410M greenfield biorefinery, industrial capex builds, and a spell applying Theory of Constraints to figure out why a steel fabricator's output was stuck.

International experience across India, Mozambique and Italy. Right to work in India and the UK.
