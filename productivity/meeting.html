Meeting time finder

The seven places I keep scheduling across, on one line — add or remove cities as you need. Slide to a time or pick one of the suggested windows. Each city keeps its own working hours, and daylight saving is worked out for the date you choose, so the weeks when Europe and the US change clocks on different Sundays come out right.
