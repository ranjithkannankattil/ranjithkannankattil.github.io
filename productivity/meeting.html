Seven places I keep scheduling across, on one line. Slide to a time or pick one of the suggested windows. Each city keeps its own working hours, and daylight saving is worked out for the date you choose, so the weeks when Europe and the US change clocks on different Sundays come out right.

Each city's working day in its own local time. Drag either end; an end earlier than the start means an overnight shift. Saved in this browser.
