Timeline & Gantt

Phases, tasks, milestones and the links between them, on one screen. Type into the list or drag the bars. When it's ready, download an Excel workbook whose bars redraw when you change a date, a PDF for the meeting, or an image for your slides. Your timelines stay in this browser.

#TaskStartEndDaysDoneOwnerAfter

An empty timeline. Add a first task, start from a template, or paste rows from a spreadsheet.

  • Drag a bar to move it, or its ends to change dates; drag the little triangle under a bar to set progress.
  • Link tasks by dragging the dot at the end of a bar onto the task that follows it.
  • Enter in a name adds the next task; Alt+↑/↓ moves a row; drag a row number to reorder; double-click a bar for all its details.
  • Ctrl + scroll over the chart zooms; drag empty space to pan.

Nothing leaves your browser unless you share a link, and the timeline travels inside that link itself. The Excel download keeps the chart live: change a start, end or done figure and the bars redraw, with today marked in red.

Paste from a spreadsheet

Copy the rows in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers, headings included, and paste them here. Columns it understands: Task or Name, Start, End or Duration, Done %, Owner, Phase, Type and Predecessors. A row with no dates becomes a phase heading; a row with only one date becomes a milestone.
