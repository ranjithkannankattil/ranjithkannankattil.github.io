Scholarship finder for Indian students

Scholarships for study in Europe, the UK, Australia, America, China, Japan, Korea and the Middle East, each linked to its official page. Choose a region and level, or add your age and work experience to hide what you can’t apply for. Nothing you enter leaves this page.

    Nothing matches those filters. Try clearing your age, work years or funding choice.

    Avoid scholarship scams

    Use the official link on each card. Do not pay anyone who says they can release an award, or cover its visa or insurance costs. The Swiss government warns about offers like these, and the same rule is a good guide everywhere.

    Check before you rely on a list

      Rules and dates change every year, and “Closed” can mean a new round is about to start. Always confirm on the official page before you apply. This is an independent guide and is not connected to any scholarship provider. Spotted a mistake? Write to hello@ranjithkb.com.
