Map colourer

Pick a colour, then click countries on the globe to fill them. Open any country to colour its states or provinces the same way. Only the places you pick take a colour; the rest stay blank, with or without borders. India is drawn as India maps it — Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh in full, Arunachal Pradesh within India. Your maps stay in this browser.

Colours

Pick a colour in the bar above the map (or press 1–9), then tap places. Tap a place again to clear it, or switch to Brush and drag across many at once. Labels show in the legend.
