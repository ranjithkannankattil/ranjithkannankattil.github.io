PDF toolkit

Drop in PDFs and photos, then arrange the pages: drag to reorder, rotate, delete, or pull a few out on their own. Add page numbers, a watermark or your signature, shrink a heavy scan, and download one PDF or split it into several. Everything happens on this device — your files are never uploaded.

Sign and fill

Make a signature once, then place it — or a name or date — on any page.

    Size

    Pages are copied exactly; text stays selectable.

    Text recognition (OCR)

    Reads the words on scanned pages and photos, so the PDF can be searched and its text copied. Pages that already have text are left as they are.

    The searchable layer works for English and Italian. For Malayalam and Hindi, use Get the text below — PDF text layers can’t yet carry those scripts reliably.

    Built on pdf-lib and Mozilla's PDF.js, both open source and loaded from this site. Text recognition uses Tesseract.js, also running on your device; the first use downloads its reading engine and language (about 7 MB), then the browser keeps it. Password-protected PDFs open once you type the password; their pages are copied as images. Smaller and Smallest redraw each page as a picture, which shrinks scans a lot but makes text unselectable.
