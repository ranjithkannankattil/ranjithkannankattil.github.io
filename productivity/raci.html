RACI matrix builder

Deliverables down the side, roles across the top. Click a cell to cycle through R, A, A/R, C and I, or move with the arrow keys and type the letter. Each row should have exactly one person accountable and at least one responsible; the right-hand column tells you when it doesn't. Your matrices stay in this browser.

Paste from a spreadsheet

Copy the block in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers — the first row holding the role names, the first column the deliverables, and R, A, C, I (or S) in the cells. A row with nothing in any cell becomes a section heading.
