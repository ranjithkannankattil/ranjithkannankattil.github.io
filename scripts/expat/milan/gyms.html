Gyms open 24/7

Milan's round-the-clock gyms are almost all Anytime Fitness clubs: members get in with a key fob at any hour, and staff are usually there only on weekday daytimes and Saturday mornings. Ratings are from the palestre.fitness review aggregator, checked October 2026.

"Not rated" means no aggregated score was found; check the club's Google reviews. Anytime memberships include access to its clubs worldwide after a short period, useful if you travel. Prices are set per club; ask about the joining fee, which can be high.

Other options (not 24/7)

  • McFIT (Città Studi, Certosa, Viale Fulvio Testi): budget chain, open 07:00–23:00 weekdays and 09:00–21:00 weekends. Flexible monthly plan about €49.90, cheaper on a 12-month contract.
  • Virgin Active: premium clubs with pools and classes across the city; long hours, but not overnight.
  • Palestre Italiane, Fit Express and many independent gyms: mid-price, neighbourhood-based.
  • Milanosport: the city's public sports centres and pools, cheapest option, with per-entry tickets.
  • FitPrime: one subscription for entries at many Milan gyms and studios, good if you move around the city.
  • Italian gyms ask for a medical certificate for non-competitive sport (certificato medico sportivo non agonistico) from your GP or a sports clinic. Bring it when you join.
