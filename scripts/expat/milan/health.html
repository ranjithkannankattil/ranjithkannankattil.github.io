Public health service (SSN) and the health card

  • Employees and their dependent family with a permit for work or family reasons have compulsory, free SSN enrolment. You get the tessera sanitaria and a family doctor; children get a paediatrician.
  • People who can't enrol compulsorily can use voluntary enrolment: since the 2024 budget law the minimum is €2,000 per year per calendar year (reduced for students and au pairs).
  • Visits to specialists need a GP referral (impegnativa) and a small fee (ticket); book through the regional booking line or the Lombardy health app. Records live in the Fascicolo Sanitario Elettronico.

Family doctor, pharmacies and booking

  • Choose or change your medico di base (GP) and your children's pediatra online in the Fascicolo Sanitario or at an ASST office. Ask colleagues which GPs speak English.
  • GP visits are free. The GP writes electronic prescriptions (ricetta elettronica) and specialist referrals (impegnativa); you pay a ticket fee for specialists and tests unless exempt.
  • Book specialist visits through the regional booking line, the SALUTILE app or the pharmacy. Waits can be long; the referral's priority code (U, B, D, P) sets the maximum wait.
  • Pharmacies (green cross) dispense, advise and measure blood pressure. There is always a night pharmacy on duty: see farmaciediturno.org.
  • Out of hours and not an emergency: call 116117 (continuità assistenziale).
  • Check live emergency-room waits in the SALUTILE Pronto Soccorso app before going (see Apps).
