  • Paper tickets ended on 1 January 2026. Tap a contactless bank card or phone at the gates (it applies the best daily fare automatically), use the ATM app, or the rechargeable RicaricaMi card.
  • Five metro lines: M1 red, M2 green, M3 yellow, M4 blue (city to Linate airport), M5 lilac (to San Siro).
  • Malpensa airport: Malpensa Express from Cadorna or Centrale (separate ticket). Linate: M4. Bergamo Orio: buses from Centrale.
  • Regional trains: Trenord (Como, Bergamo, Monza, lakes). High-speed: Trenitalia Frecciarossa and Italo; book early for cheaper fares.
  • Strikes (sciopero) are announced in advance, usually on Fridays or Mondays, with guaranteed service hours. Check the ATM app the night before.
  • Bikes and scooters: BikeMi (city bike sharing), plus app-based e-bikes, e-scooters and moped sharing. Taxis can't be hailed easily; use a rank, call, or the it.Taxi / FREENOW apps.

Taxis from Malpensa and Linate to the city run at fixed fares; ask before you get in.

Travel tips

  • Tap the same card or phone in and out of every metro; using two cards breaks the daily cap.
  • Night service: metro runs until about midnight–00:30; night buses (linee notturne) replace it, and some lines run all night on Saturdays.
  • The Passante (S-lines) is part of the urban network inside Milan; useful between Garibaldi, Repubblica, Porta Venezia and Rogoredo.
  • Monthly passes are personal: the card has your photo and must match your ID.
