Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

In short: this is a personal website. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your settings (favourite stations, volume, default language, news places and languages, meeting-finder cities) stay in your own browser. A visitor counter keeps one shared total — no names, addresses or identifiers. The only thing stored about you anywhere is what you choose to save by signing in on the Radio page — your Google account ID and email, your favourite stations and your default language — and you can have that removed at any time.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Ranjith Kannankattil, an individual based in Milan, Italy, who is the data controller for anything described here. To ask a question or exercise any of your rights, message @runjyth on Instagram.

What stays in your browser

Some pages remember your choices using your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device and is not sent to me:

  • Radio — favourite stations, the last station played, volume, your default language and recently used languages.
  • News — the place you last looked at, your list of places, the languages you picked and whether other outlets are shown.
  • Productivity — the cities, working hours and meeting title you set in the meeting finder.
  • Every page — the date your browser was last counted by the visitor counter.

You can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Visitor counter

The number at the bottom of each page is a single running total of visits. Each browser adds one, at most once a day. To avoid counting you twice, your browser keeps the date it last counted in its own local storage; that date never leaves your device. The total itself is stored in Google Firebase (EU) as one number — nothing about who visited, from where, or what they read. As with any web request, Google's servers see your IP address when the number is updated, but it is not stored with the count.

Signing in on the Radio page (optional)

If you choose Sign in to save favourites, you sign in with your Google account so your favourites follow you to other devices.

WhatWhy
Your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture link (held by Firebase Authentication)To know which saved list is yours when you sign in. Your name is shown only to you, next to the Sign out button.

  • Legal basis: you ask for this feature by signing in, so it is processed to provide it to you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Without signing in, everything still works and nothing is stored about you.
  • Who processes it: Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited), with data stored in the EU (Firestore location eur3). Google acts as a processor under its data processing terms.
  • Who can see it: only you, through the site; the database rules let each signed-in person read and change only their own record. I can see it as the site owner, and look at it only to fix problems or to delete it when asked.
  • How long: until you ask for it to be deleted. Records not used for two years may be deleted.
  • It is never sold, shared for marketing or used for anything else.

Services your browser connects to

Like most websites, pages here load some parts from other services. Each one receives your IP address and basic browser information when it does:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this site (GitHub keeps standard server logs).
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — the player library used for some radio streams.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) — serves the News page's headlines, which are rebuilt every two hours.
  • Newspapers — only when you open a story, which takes you to that paper's own website (occasionally through a Google News link). Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Radio broadcasters — when you press play, your browser connects directly to that station's streaming server, as it would on the station's own website. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Google Firebase — to update and show the visitor total, and if you sign in, as described above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the data held about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to its use. Message @runjyth on Instagram and it will be handled within 30 days. Signing out does not delete your saved list — ask, and it will be removed. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Children

Signing in is not intended for children under 14 (the age of digital consent in Italy).

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top will change with it.
