Indonesian restaurants are rare in Milan. Cingapura (above) is the closest, and the Indonesian community's events are where home food usually appears (see Communities).

Milanese food to try

  • Risotto alla milanese (saffron), often served with ossobuco (braised veal shank).
  • Cotoletta alla milanese: a breaded veal chop on the bone, fried in butter.
  • Cassoeula in winter: pork and savoy cabbage stew.
  • Panzerotti from Luini near the Duomo, and panettone at Christmas.
  • Aperitivo from about 18:00 to 21:00: a drink comes with snacks, sometimes a full buffet. Isola, Navigli and Porta Venezia are the busy areas.
  • Book ahead on Friday and Saturday nights. Many places close one day a week (often Sunday evening or Monday) and much of August.
