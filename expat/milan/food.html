Eat Milanese: risotto alla milanese (saffron), cotoletta, ossobuco, Luini's panzerotti near the Duomo, panettone at Christmas, and aperitivo (a drink with snacks, 18:00–21:00) in Isola, Navigli or Porta Venezia. Check recent reviews before going; restaurants change.
