Install these first

Most Italian public apps ask you to sign in with SPID or CIE, so set up your digital identity before anything else. Each app links to its App Store or Google Play page; where that could not be confirmed, to the official website, which has its own download buttons.

PosteID (SPID)The most common SPID identity; free, set up at a post office or online.iPhoneAndroidSite

Phone and internet

All SIMs need your passport and codice fiscale. Prepaid plans with 100–200 GB cost about €7–12 a month. Home fibre (FTTH) is widely available in Milan for about €25–30 a month; check coverage by address.
