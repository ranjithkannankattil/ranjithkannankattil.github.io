Communities

Where to meet people: expat networks, nationality associations, chambers of commerce and free Italian classes.

Checked November 2026 · rules change, so confirm fees and deadlines on the linked official pages

Communities, for every nationality

Everyone

  • InterNations Milan: big monthly expat events and nationality sub-groups.
  • Meetup: language exchanges, hiking, running, board games, tech.
  • Facebook groups such as "Expats in Milan" and "Milan Expats": flats, questions, second-hand. Reddit: r/milano, r/italy, r/expats.
  • Easy Milano: English-language listings, schools, events.
  • Business: British Chamber of Commerce for Italy, American Chamber of Commerce in Italy, Indo-Italian Chamber of Commerce.
  • Free Italian classes for foreign residents at CPIA state adult-education centres; the Comune also lists cheap courses. This is the fastest way to meet people from everywhere.
CommunityWhere to start
Indian (all states)Consulate General of India Milan cultural events (Diwali, Independence Day, Yoga Day); Indian associations and student groups at Politecnico and Bocconi; Via Padova and Corso Buenos Aires shops
MalayaliIndian Malayalee Cultural Association of Milan (Onam celebrations); Adlers Lombard FC, a Malayali football club in Lombardy
IndonesianKMIM – Komunitas Muslim Indonesia Milano; PPI Milano (Indonesian students); Mawar Sharon Church Milan; events by KBRI Roma and the Indonesian trade office (ITPC) in Milan
Sri LankanConsulate General of Sri Lanka, Milan; Sri Lankan community is large in Milan; Serendib restaurant hosts cultural evenings
FilipinoOne of Milan's largest Asian communities; Philippine Consulate General in Milan and parish-based groups
ChinesePaolo Sarpi (Chinatown); Chinese associations and the Consulate General
Pakistani · BangladeshiLarge communities around Via Padova and the north-east; community associations and mosques
British · American · AustralianChambers of commerce, international school parent groups, InterNations
Other nationalitiesYour consulate in Milan (most countries have one) usually knows the community associations. Ask for their newsletter.
