What to carry to every appointment

  • Passport plus a photocopy of every used page, and the visa page.
  • Permit card or the post-office ricevuta while you wait for it.
  • Codice fiscale (the card or the printed certificate).
  • Registered rental contract (or a dichiarazione di ospitalità if you live with someone) and the residence certificate once you have it.
  • Employment contract, the last 3 payslips and the employer's details.
  • 4 recent passport photos (white background, 35×45 mm). The site's passport photo maker makes a print sheet.
  • A €16 marca da bollo (revenue stamp) from any tabaccheria; many forms need one.
  • Originals and copies of apostilled certificates (birth, marriage, degrees) with sworn Italian translations.

Keep scans of everything in one cloud folder. Offices often ask for a document you showed the week before.
