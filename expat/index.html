Before any move

  • Apostille and translate birth, marriage and degree certificates before you leave. It is slow and expensive from abroad.
  • Scan everything into one cloud folder: passports, visas, contracts, payslips, medical records.
  • Keep your home phone number alive (roaming or eSIM) so bank and government OTPs still reach you.
  • Bring 3–6 months of costs: deposits, agency fees and the gap before the first salary add up.
  • Sort health cover from day one, until the local public system enrols you.
  • Learn 50 words of the local language before you land. Greeting people in their language opens more doors than anything else.
